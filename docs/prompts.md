# Prompt ideas

The default ComfyUI and web-app prompt is:

> A luminous abstract performance stage made from hand-cut paper shapes, cobalt blue and orange light, energetic theatrical composition

Try changing one visual idea at a time while keeping the source gesture or pose steady:

- **Paper theater:** A tiny paper theater built from layered cut-paper arches, cobalt and vermilion light, bold shadows, handmade stage design
- **Gesture creature:** A translucent creature made from flowing ribbons of ink, following the silhouette and gesture, vivid colors on a dark stage
- **Weather machine:** A dreamlike weather machine made of glass, moss, and copper, theatrical spotlights, surreal stage prop
- **Chalk garden:** An expressive chalk drawing of oversized flowers growing through a geometric city, joyful movement, limited palette
- **Puppet portrait:** A handmade marionette portrait assembled from painted wood and fabric, dramatic side light, textured stop-motion aesthetic
- **Liquid architecture:** A monumental liquid-glass structure bending around the input pose, iridescent reflections, performance-art lighting

For image-to-image or control modes, describe the desired transformation rather than repeating every detail visible in the guide. Short prompts often leave more room for the live source to shape the result.
